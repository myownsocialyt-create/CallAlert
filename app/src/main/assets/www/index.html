How to use

  1. Add your vehicle number in My Garage.
  2. Tap Go online so people can reach you. You stay reachable while the app is open.
  3. Open Windshield card, save or share it, print it and place it on your dashboard.
  4. Anyone who needs you scans the QR code, or types your vehicle number in this app, and taps call.
  5. Your phone number is never displayed or shared.

Tip: keep the app open in the background while parked. Android may close background apps to save battery — if you were closed, simply reopen the app and go online again.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Vehicle Call Alert has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising SDKs. We do not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your vehicle numbers, nicknames, app settings and call history are saved only in this app's local storage. Uninstalling the app or tapping "Delete all app data" removes them permanently.

Microphone

Microphone access is used only while a voice call is active, so the other person can hear you. Audio is never recorded, stored or uploaded. Calls are peer-to-peer and encrypted in transit (DTLS-SRTP).

Network

To connect a call the app contacts a public signalling server (PeerJS) and public STUN servers. These services see the vehicle number used as a connection ID and your network address, which is required to set up any internet call. No call audio passes through our servers.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can revoke microphone access at any time in Android Settings, and delete all local data from the Settings screen in this app.

About
